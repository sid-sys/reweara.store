Shipping Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Where we deliver

We deliver across India, to PIN codes that our courier partners serve. At checkout we tell you if your PIN code is not served.

2. How shipping works

  1. You place a prepaid order and pay online.
  2. The seller packs the item and ships it within [48 HOURS].
  3. The parcel goes with a courier partner. The seller either hands it over at pickup or books the courier as shown in the app.
  4. You get a tracking link and order updates until delivery.

3. Ways a parcel can be sent

The seller uses one of these ways. The order page shows which one applies.

  1. Courier pickup. A courier partner comes to the seller's address and collects the parcel. We make the shipping label. This is our preferred way where pickup is available.
  2. Seller drops the parcel. The seller takes the packed parcel with our label to a courier office or post office that accepts it.
  3. Seller ships on their own. The seller sends the parcel with a courier or India Post of their choice. The seller enters the courier name and the tracking number on Reweara. We check the number, and we may ask for a photo of the shipping receipt.

In every case the buyer gets a tracking link.

4. Delivery time

Delivery usually takes [3 TO 7 WORKING DAYS] after dispatch. The estimate is shown on the order page. Delays can happen because of weather, strikes or courier problems.

5. Shipping fee

The shipping fee is shown before you pay. We accept prepaid orders only. We do not offer Cash on Delivery.

6. Failed delivery

If the courier cannot reach you or the address is wrong, they try again. If delivery still fails, the parcel may return to the seller. Please give a full address, PIN code, a landmark and a working phone number.

7. Damaged or lost parcels

If your parcel is damaged or lost, contact us. See the Refund, Return and Cancellation Policy.
