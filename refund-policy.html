Refund, Return and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Items on REWEARA are pre-owned and each one is a single piece. This policy explains when you can cancel, return or get money back. REWEARA reviews every case. Sellers do not decide refunds on their own.

1. Cancellation

  • Buyer: you can cancel an order before the seller ships it. Your full payment is refunded.
  • After shipping: the order cannot be cancelled. If the parcel is not as described, see section 2.
  • Seller: if a seller does not ship within [48 HOURS], we may cancel the order and refund the buyer in full.

2. Returns and refunds

You can ask for a return or refund if:

  • The item is very different from the listing (size, colour, brand, condition).
  • The item is fake or counterfeit.
  • The item is damaged, stained or has defects that the seller did not show.
  • You received the wrong item.
  • The parcel is lost in transit.

Not accepted: change of mind, wrong size ordered when the listing size was correct, or normal signs of use that the listing showed.

3. How to raise a problem

  1. Report the problem within [48 HOURS] of delivery. Use the order page or write to [SUPPORT EMAIL].
  2. Add clear photos or a short video. Include the item label and the parcel.
  3. We review your request and may ask the seller for their side.
  4. We tell you the result within [3 WORKING DAYS].

4. If a return is approved

  • We arrange the return pickup, or tell you how to send the item back.
  • When the seller receives the item, your refund is started.
  • For fake, damaged or wrongly described items, return shipping is paid by the seller or by us, not by you.
  • For a lost parcel, you get a full refund and we handle the courier claim.

5. How and when you get your money

  • Refunds go back to the same payment method you used.
  • Time to appear in your account: usually [5 TO 7 WORKING DAYS] after approval. Your bank may take extra time.

6. Delivery failed because of the buyer

If delivery fails because of a wrong address or because the buyer could not be reached, the parcel may return to the seller. In this case the refund may be reduced by shipping charges.
