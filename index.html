How it works

Simple for sellers. Easy for buyers.

1

Sellers list

Take clear photos. Add size, brand, condition and price. Tell buyers about any stain or repair.

2

Buyers order

Pick what you like. Enter your address. Pay online. Get updates about your order.

3

Delivered, with tracking

The parcel is shipped with a courier. Buyers get a tracking link and follow it until it arrives.

Built on trust

Second-hand only works when people can trust each other.

That is why we plan to build safety in from day one, not add it later.

  • Verified sellers. Phone and ID checks before a seller can be paid.
  • Real photos. Sellers show the front, the back and the labels.
  • Honest condition. New with tags, like new, good or fair.
  • Tracked delivery. One tracking link for every parcel.
  • Fair problem handling. If an item is not as described, we help sort it out.

About the business

What Reweara is

Reweara is an online marketplace for pre-owned (used) clothes in India. Individual sellers list clothes they own. Buyers pay on Reweara.

Reweara does not make or sell its own clothes.

Read our Terms, Refund, Return and Cancellation Policy, Shipping Policy and Privacy Policy.
