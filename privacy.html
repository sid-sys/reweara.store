REWEARA ("we", "us") is a marketplace at reweara.store where people in India sell and buy pre-owned clothes. We run it as [YOUR BUSINESS NAME], a sole proprietorship. This page explains what personal information we collect and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email address.
  • Delivery details: address, PIN code, phone number.
  • Seller details: PAN, identity document, bank account details, pickup address. These are used to verify sellers and to pay them.
  • Listings: photos and descriptions you upload.
  • Order and payment records: what was bought, amount, payment status. We do not store your card number, UPI PIN or net-banking password. Payments are handled by our payment partner.
  • Technical data: device type, IP address and pages visited.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account and process orders.
  • To verify sellers and prevent fraud.
  • To pay sellers and process refunds.
  • To arrange delivery and send order updates (SMS, email, app messages).
  • To answer questions and solve disputes.
  • To obey the law.

3. Who we share it with

  • Payment partner (for example PhonePe Payment Gateway) to take payments and pay sellers.
  • Courier and shipping partners to pick up and deliver parcels. They get the name, address and phone number needed for delivery.
  • Identity verification partners to check seller identity.
  • The other side of an order. A seller sees the buyer's name and delivery address for that order. A buyer sees the seller's public profile.
  • Authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as the law requires for accounts, tax and disputes. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

5. Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information.
  • You can stop marketing emails at any time.
  • To make a request, write to us at the email below.

6. Security

We use reasonable steps to protect your information. No system is perfectly safe, so please keep your login private.

7. Children
